Registrations and disclosures

Who we are, what we are registered for, and how we are paid.

Registrations

Our AMFI registration covers mutual fund distribution only. Each other service has its own regulator and its own registration or partner.

Mutual Funds

Our role: AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor, ARN-362628

Product regulator: SEBI (schemes run by SEBI-registered AMCs)

Portfolio Management Services

Our role: Registration, number and partner to confirm

Product regulator: SEBI (portfolio managers)

Alternative Investment Funds

Our role: Registration, number and partner to confirm

Corporate Invoice Discounting

Our role: Registration, number and partner to confirm

Our role: Registration, number and partner to confirm

Bonds

How we are paid

As a distributor we receive commission from the product providers whose products you invest in through us. Full commission disclosure, conflicts and how they are managed

Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks, read all scheme related documents carefully. Past performance is not indicative of future returns.

Returns mentioned for invoice discounting, P2P lending, bonds, NCDs and MLDs are indicative and not guaranteed. PMS, AIFs, P2P lending, invoice discounting and MLDs carry higher risk and lower liquidity and may not suit all investors.

Insurance is the subject matter of solicitation. Tax treatment is based on current law and may change; please consult your tax professional.
