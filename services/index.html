Mutual Funds

Diversified investing, from a small monthly SIP.

Talk to us
Eligibility & minimum

Resident and NRI investors with KYC

SIP from ₹100–₹500 a month; lump sum from ₹500–₹5,000 (varies by scheme)

Tenure & liquidity

No fixed tenure

Open-ended schemes redeemable on any business day

ELSS: 3-year lock-in

Return structure

Market-linked; not guaranteed

Keep in mind: Market, credit, interest-rate and liquidity risk; returns can be negative.

Portfolio Management Services

Eligibility & minimum

₹50 lakh in cash or securities; accredited investors may be exempt

Tenure & liquidity

No regulatory lock-in; exit loads may apply per agreement

Suggested horizon 3–5 years+

Return structure

Market-linked; not guaranteed

Fixed, performance or hybrid fees

Keep in mind: Concentrated portfolios; manager risk; market risk.

P2P Lending

Monthly inflows from lending across many verified borrowers.

Talk to us
Eligibility & minimum

Up to ₹50 lakh across all platforms (RBI); over ₹10 lakh needs a CA net-worth certificate (₹50 lakh+)

Tenure & liquidity

12–14 months; repaid in monthly instalments; early exit generally not available

Return structure

Approx. 13–14% IRR, indicative and not assured Source and date to confirm

Keep in mind: Defaults and delays; platforms don’t guarantee repayment. Monthly receipts include your own principal.

Bonds

Predictable coupons from government, PSU and corporate issuers.

Talk to us
Eligibility & minimum

From ₹10,000 for most listed and government bonds; varies by issue

Tenure & liquidity

91 days (T-Bills) to 30+ years

Listed bonds can be sold, but liquidity varies

Return structure

Fixed or floating coupon; price moves with interest rates if sold early

Keep in mind: Credit, interest-rate, liquidity, call and reinvestment risk.

NCDs

Fixed-rate corporate debentures with regular payouts.

Talk to us
Eligibility & minimum

Typically ₹10,000 in public issues; private placements vary

Tenure & liquidity

Usually 1–10 years

Listed NCDs can be sold on the exchange

Return structure

Fixed coupon

Monthly, quarterly, annual or cumulative payout

Keep in mind: Issuer credit risk; “secured” does not guarantee full recovery.

MLDs

Debentures with returns linked to a market index.

Talk to us
Eligibility & minimum

Face value of ₹1 lakh per debenture

Tenure & liquidity

Usually 12–60 months; low liquidity, best held to maturity

Return structure

Depends on index performance and the payoff in the term sheet

Keep in mind: Principal protection depends on type and on the issuer repaying.
